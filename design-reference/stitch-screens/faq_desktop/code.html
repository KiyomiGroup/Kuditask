It is a micro-task marketplace where Taskers complete digital tasks for rewards. Clients submit task requests through the platform's administrative process.

account_tree Client requests pass through escrow verification before assignment to the active queue.

Anyone who meets the platform's registration and participation requirements can apply to become a Tasker.

Choose an available task, start it, complete the instructions, and submit valid proof before your reservation expires.

Starting a task reserves one slot for 10 minutes. Complete the task and submit your proof within that reservation period.

timer Unsubmitted reservations auto-expire back to the community pool at 00:00.

Screenshots provide proof that the requested action was completed and allow the platform to verify your submission.

The reward for that submission will not be confirmed. A rejection does not automatically mean you committed fraud.

Fake, edited, duplicated, or misleading proof can result in the reward being withheld and disciplinary action. Repeated fraudulent submissions can affect your account and tier progression.

Task rewards depend on your current Tasker tier. Tier 1 starts at ₦10 per verified task.

Rewards are added to your available balance after the submitted proof has been verified.

The minimum withdrawal amount is ₦5,000.

You must have at least 500 verified completed tasks and 10 active login days. Both requirements must be met.

Requirement 1 500 Tasks Verified & Approved
Requirement 2 10 Days Active login history

Withdrawals are processed every Sunday at 5:00 PM.

The platform has 100 Tasker tiers. Higher tiers provide higher rewards per verified task.

Tier 100 pays ₦510 per verified task.

Tier 1 pays ₦10, Tier 2 pays ₦20, and each tier after Tier 2 increases the reward by ₦5.

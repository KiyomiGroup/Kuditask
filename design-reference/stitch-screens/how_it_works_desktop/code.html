ANTI-HOARDING PROTOCOL

WHY IS THERE A 10-MINUTE TIMER?

When you start a task, your slot is temporarily reserved for 10 minutes. Complete the task and submit your proof within that period. If the timer expires, the slot is released for another tasker.

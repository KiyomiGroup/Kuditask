Frequently Addressed Queries

timelapse

Why does my task have a 10-minute lock?

When you click "Claim Task", it is reserved exclusively for your account for exactly 10 minutes. If proof is not uploaded before the timer reaches 00:00, the task returns to the public pool to ensure fair distribution.

hourglass_top

What is the difference between Verified and Pending Balance?

Pending Balance refers to earnings from submitted tasks currently under automated OCR or manual advertiser review. Once accepted, funds instantly move into your Verified Balance, eligible for Sunday disbursement.

fact_check

How does screenshot OCR audit work?

Our optical character recognition scans the exact timestamps, target account handles, and transaction references within your uploaded image. Ensure crops don't cut off your mobile status bar or username.

calendar_month

When can I place my withdrawal request?

Withdrawals run automatically every Sunday at 5:00 PM WAT once you achieve 500 completed tasks across at least 10 active days. You do not need to manually request payout if your Nigerian bank account is verified.

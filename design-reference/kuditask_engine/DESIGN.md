## Brand & Style
The design system reflects a financial-grade digital workplace engineered specifically for Nigeria’s agile digital labor force. It bridges high-velocity micro-tasking with enterprise fintech security, evoking the institutional dependability of modern African payment infrastructure alongside the crisp momentum of an active income engine.

### Core Visual Principles
- **Institutional Reliability:** The interface behaves with the structural rigor of a core-banking dashboard. Interfaces must communicate zero ambiguity regarding task payouts, escrow statuses, and currency disbursement.
- **Vibrant Velocity:** Energetic mint accents cut through deep corporate emeralds, drawing instantaneous attention to actionable items: high-value tasks, expiring timer locks, tier advancements, and real-time wallet settlements.
- **Information Density with Breathing Room:** Designed for swift decision-making across varied screen sizes and low-bandwidth connections. Dense financial data and queue tables are paired with unambiguous typographic contrast and strict layout discipline.
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Corporate Modern integrated with Tactile-Fintech cues. Card surfaces utilize crisp structural borders, slight surface shifts, and minimal ambient drop shadows to produce an interface that feels tangible, responsive, and definitive.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional integrity with active monetary rewards. Deep forest tones anchor core structural components, while vibrant accents spotlight liquidity and urgent tasks.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#054F31`):** Deep Forest Emerald. Anchor color applied to top navigation bars, key headers, balance containers, active states, and dominant visual weight elements.
- **Secondary (`#00875A`):** Classic Nigerian Standard Emerald. Applied to primary call-to-actions, successful status tags, and confirmation flows.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Mint Accent. Highlights active state progress indicators, live task tickers, and interactive badges.
- **Neutral Surface (`#0F172A`):** Slate Navy. Used for primary typography, crisp vector icons, and foundational dark containers.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering:**
  - Base Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Card & Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Muted Insets & Table Rows: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Subtle Dividing Lines: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
  - Muted Secondary Body Text: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Payout Settlement / Verified: `#059669` (Emerald 600) with `#ECFDF5` (Emerald 50) background.
  - Task Countdown / Escalation Warning: `#D97706` (Amber 600) with `#FFFBEB` (Amber 50) background.
  - Disputed / Failed Verification: `#DC2626` (Crimson 600) with `#FEF2F2` (Red 50) background.
  - Info / Review State: `#2563EB` (Blue 600) with `#EFF6FF` (Blue 50) background.

## Typography
Typographic scale unites technical precision with high-legibility interface design.

### Font Pairing Strategy
- **Display & Financial Counters (`Space Grotesk`):** Modern geometric grotesk with distinct tabular qualities. Selected for numbers, wallet counters, the Nigerian Naira symbol (`₦`), system statuses, badges, and primary sectional anchors.
- **Interactive UI & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, highly legible, human-centric sans-serif optimized for long task guidelines, submission checklists, admin logs, and operational forms.

### Nigerian Naira (`₦`) Currency Display Rules
- The Naira symbol must always match the font family and weight of the adjacent numeral amount.
- In financial headers (`currency-display`), render the sign in `#10B981` or `#00875A` while the numeric string remains `#0F172A` to provide instant balance recognition.
- Negative adjustments or deduction previews always anchor with a minus prefix before the symbol: `-₦1,200.00`.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column layout handles data-rich queues, adaptive cards, and modular wallet widgets.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Canvas margin: `1rem` (`16px`). Task cards stack full-width; bottom navigation bar anchors primary actions.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Canvas margin: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Task feed expands to 2-column grids; wallet headers shift to side-by-side metric containers.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Canvas margin: `2.5rem` (`40px`). Maximum content width clamped to `1280px` for optimal eye tracking across administration tables.

### Structural Densities
- Vertical spacing relies on strict `0.5rem` (`8px`) increments.
- Wallet balances, active task timers, and validation warnings observe atomic containment: dense inside (`space-sm` to `space-md`), generous outside (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through structured tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter lines rather than heavy drop shadows, maintaining clarity across diverse display hardware.

### Layering Rules
- **Base Ground (`#F8FAFC`):** Application canvas underpinning all screens.
- **Level 1 Surface (Cards, Modules, Feeds):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` structural outline in `#E2E8F0`. Soft ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 Surface (Dropdowns, Floating Drawers, Modals):** White (`#FFFFFF`) bound by `1px` `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active State Elevation (Hovered Task Card):** Border transitions to `#10B981` with ambient lift: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(5, 79, 49, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
A unified radius scale (`roundedness: 2`) gives inputs, panels, and badges a crisp, modern finish without feeling overly casual.

### Geometry Usage
- **Cards, Modals, Feed Modules:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`).
- **Form Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns:** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- **System Tags, Status Badges, Timer Containers:** `rounded-full` (`9999px`) for quick visual scanning.
- **Task Verification Media & Proof Containers:** Strict `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) with subtle inset outline to demarcate uploaded documents and transaction screenshots.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary (Action):** Surface `#054F31`, label `#FFFFFF`, bold `Space Grotesk`. Hover: `#00875A`. Focus ring: `2px` `#10B981` with `2px` white offset.
- **Secondary (Mint Accent / Fast Claim):** Surface `#10B981`, label `#054F31`, weight `700`. Used for direct payout claims and immediate task reservations.
- **Outline (Admin / Filtering):** Surface transparent, border `1.5px` `#E2E8F0`, label `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.

### 2. Task Cards & Reservation Countdown
- **Architecture:** Bounded by `1px` `#E2E8F0` border. Top row hosts the task category badge, dynamic payout (`₦450.00`), and real-time reservation countdown badge.
- **Countdown Badge:** Pill shape with dual indicators (Lucide `Clock` icon + `MM:SS` monospace display). Transitions to amber (`#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text) at under 5 minutes; flashes crimson (`#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text) at under 60 seconds.

### 3. Tier Progress Component (Tiers 1–100)
- **Visual Meter:** Compact multi-segment horizontal bar. Completed progress highlighted in `#10B981`, remaining track `#E2E8F0`.
- **Context Tag:** Space Grotesk pill badge (`Tier 12`, `Tier 85`) displaying small verification star icon. Shows level name alongside XP threshold (`1,420 / 2,000 XP to Tier 13`).

### 4. Wallet Breakdown Widget
- **Triple Horizon Grid:** Divided into Available Balance, Pending Escrow, and Lifetime Earnings.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** 
  - Available Balance uses `#054F31` background, `#FFFFFF` text, and large mint Naira typography (`currency-display`).
  - Pending Escrow uses `#F8FAFC` background with an amber status dot (`₦` amount subdued).
  - Lifetime Earnings uses light emerald card with a success indicator.

### 5. Form Fields & Proof Upload Insets
- **Inputs:** Height `48px`. Baseline border `1.5px` `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus border `#00875A` with `3px` mint glow (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).
- **Proof-of-Work Dropzones:** Dashed border `2px` `#94A3B8`, background `#F8FAFC`. Displays upload guidelines for fast verification and processing.

### 6. Admin Operational Queue Table
- **Data Density:** Low vertical padding (`10px 16px`), alternating subtle striped rows (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`).
- **Audit Badges:** Direct dual-status tags (`Under Review`, `Approved`, `Flagged for Fraud`) paired with verification action toggles and inline ID copying.
